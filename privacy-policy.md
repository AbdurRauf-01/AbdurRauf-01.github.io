# Privacy Policy for Drive Sync

**Last updated:** October 20, 2026

Drive Sync ("the App") is a personal, single-user tool used to sync files between the developer's own computer and the developer's own Google Drive account using rclone. It is not offered to the public.

## Who operates the App

Abdur Rauf ("I", "me"). Contact: 0h0kgsuss@mozmail.com

## Data the App accesses

When I authorize the App with my Google account, it can access files and metadata in my own Google Drive, using the Google Drive API scope I grant during sign-in. This is used only to list, upload, download, and sync my own files.

## How data is used

- Data is used solely to perform file synchronization between my Google Drive and my local device.
- Data is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose other than syncing.

## Data storage

- Synced files are stored on my own device.
- OAuth tokens are stored locally in my rclone configuration file on my device.
- The App has no server, database, or backend. No Google user data is transmitted to or stored by me or any third party.

## Data sharing

I do not sell, share, transfer, or disclose Google user data to anyone. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Data retention and deletion

Data remains on my device until I delete it. I can revoke the App's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the local rclone configuration to remove stored tokens.

## Children

The App is not directed at children and is not offered to anyone other than its developer.
